# Collective Action: reviewed consumer boycotts

Entry points: Circles → Collective Action, and Work & Worker Support → Explore Collective Action. Route: `#circles/action`.

Members with active, admitted profiles can submit company-focused proposals with documented claims, a specific demand, uncertainties/responses, alternatives and financial interests, one to five HTTPS sources, and a future review date within 90 days. Proposals are not published automatically. No example accusations, real campaigns, partners, or memberships are seeded.

Existing authorized reviewers access a Review view in this area. Independent review uses the app's connection/block conflict checks and prohibits self-approval, including by the owner. A reviewer records an explanation and confirms source/safety review before approval, renewal, rejection, pause, completion, or feedback disposition. There is no automatic claim-verification service. Hosting does not imply platform endorsement.

Participation records are durable and private to the participant through the API. No roster, aggregate count, feed event, friendship-profile field, or push notification is produced. Authorized system operators still have database access. Joining records intent; purchases are not tracked. Leaving and ownership-checked withdrawal remain available to suspended or paused members. Withdrawal retries are idempotent. Active participants can submit concerns about campaigns whose review dates have expired. Deleting an account removes its participation, redacts its feedback, and withdraws/redacts its proposals while removing participation attached to those proposals.

Corrections, responses, concerns and progress submissions go into a private review queue. Submitters can see their own feedback on accessible campaigns; campaign proposers cannot see others' feedback unless independently authorized as reviewers. Reviewers publish their reviewed summary, not raw submissions. Published decisions remain in the campaign history. Restricted access is not a promise of anonymity, confidentiality or end-to-end encryption. No response SLA is promised.

Past-due active campaigns disappear from general discovery and reject new participation on the server. Their proposers, existing participants and reviewers can still access them. Renewing requires a new future review date. Reviewer writes use version checks and an atomic batch with a unique per-request event as an ownership token. A stale request creates no event, changes no campaign and leaves feedback open. All writes require same-origin JSON and an exact account identifier; existing quotas, admission and reviewer checks apply. Source links use HTTPS and no-referrer/noopener. Links are not fetched or embedded by the server.

The area supports consumer purchasing choices; it does not implement strikes, formal union representation, fundraising, professional advice, verified company representatives, or public user-generated campaign posting. Explore, My activity and Review use separate server-filtered lists with cursor pagination (25 campaigns per page). Expiration filtering happens before paging; personal participation is not crowded out by discovery. Campaign text cannot be directly edited after submission: corrections use the review queue; a materially revised proposal can be withdrawn and replaced subject to the proposal quota.

Schema: additive migration 0018 adds campaigns, private participation, feedback and review updates. Existing migration 0017 (worker support) is retained. Production migration is handled by Sites publication.

Verification: the SQLite-backed campaign integration suite covers authentication/origin/account checks, invalid links/dates, proposal privacy, independent authorization, stale reviews, private and idempotent joining, feedback privacy/publication, pause/expiry, leaving while suspended, withdrawal, and account deletion. All 38 automated test files passed and TypeScript validation passed. Browser automation was unavailable: responsive appearance and real interactive browser journeys have not been visually verified.

## Campaign reliability update

Regression tests reproduce withdrawal during review and verify zero review side effects, paused/suspended owner withdrawal, unauthorized withdrawal denial, idempotent retries, expiry-before-pagination, separate personal activity, tied-timestamp cursor pagination, malformed cursors and reviewer authorization. List requests are guarded against stale responses. If a mutation succeeds but refreshing fails, the interface explicitly says the change was saved and offers a reload. No data migration or added service is required.
