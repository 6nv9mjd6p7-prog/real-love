# Union organizing in Real Love

Open **Circles → Start union organizing**, or **Work & Worker Support → Start union organizing**. The direct app route is `#circles/organize`.

This creates an organizing **peer circle**. It does not register a union, appoint a representative, collect authorization cards, submit a recognition petition, or establish legal representation. The external organizer link opens an independent provider; Real Love sends no referral or member data.

An active, admitted member chooses an available accepted connection as co-founder, adds a broad group name, purpose, admission rules and a first activity, and acknowledges worker-circle privacy. The proposal stays with its two founders until the second founder reviews and agrees. Both hosts then review admission requests. New members receive conversation history from admission onward.

The `worker_organizing` category is saved by the backend independently of editable topic tags. These listings appear in worker discovery, not general friendship discovery. Membership and activity are not added to friendship profiles. Existing reporting, blocking, and admission controls remain in effect.

After approval the listing is discoverable to eligible members browsing worker circles. This is **not a confidential workplace-specific organizing tool**. Unlisted circles, pseudonymous worker identities, workplace verification, representation and retaliation protection are not implemented. Use broad industry/local descriptions and avoid identifying workplace details.

The formal-organizing contact route was checked against the AFL-CIO organizer page on October 12, 2026. Its role and availability must be checked with the organization. Legal eligibility depends on jurisdiction and employment context; the NLRB employee-rights resource describes coverage and exclusions for the United States.
