# Union organizing in Real Love

Open **Circles → Start union organizing**, or **Work & Worker Support → Start union organizing**. Direct route: `#circles/organize`.

This creates an organizing **peer circle**. It does not register a union, appoint a representative, collect authorization cards, submit a recognition petition, or establish legal representation. External organizer links open independent providers; Real Love sends no referral or member data.

An active, admitted member chooses an available accepted connection as co-founder, adds a broad group name, purpose, admission rules and an optional first activity, and acknowledges worker-circle privacy. Only the founders see the pending proposal. Both founders must agree before conversation begins.

The `worker_organizing` category stays unlisted after approval. Founders, admitted participants, applicants already on file, and named recipients of current private invitations can see the listing. Organizing circles do not accept new unsolicited join requests. Hosts can review requests already on file and privately invite accepted connections. Each named invitee reviews the disclosure and consents before joining; message history starts at admission. Private invitations expire after seven days.

Membership and activity are not added to friendship profiles. Unlisted does not mean anonymous or confidential. Use broad descriptions and avoid identifying workplace details. Existing reporting, blocking, host controls and admission restrictions remain in effect. Workplace verification, pseudonymous identities, formal representation and retaliation protection are not offered.

The AFL-CIO organizer route was checked October 12, 2026. Check its role and availability directly with the organization. The NLRB resource describes US employee-rights coverage and exclusions.
